Split Cell on the Board

Lane L2 of Layout Free. A split doubles the monitor's grid along the split (5×2 becomes 10×2), the split cell becomes two halves, and every other window keeps its size on two finer cells. S, a right-click and the inspector's Split Cell button open the "choose how" menu; while question S is open it offers halves only. Dashed pink labels mark what is new, dashed grey labels what exists today, and dotted yellow labels what is only proposed. Each option has a switch under "Show" that hides it.
