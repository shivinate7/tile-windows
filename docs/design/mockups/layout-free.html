Split Cell and Free Layout

Two owner asks of 2026-11-13. Split Cell cuts one cell in two, so a window keeps one half and the other half is empty. Free Layout lets the owner resize windows off the grid, with no gap left between them. Each option has its own drawings; the switches under "Show" hide one option. Dashed pink labels mark what is new; dashed grey labels mark what exists today.
