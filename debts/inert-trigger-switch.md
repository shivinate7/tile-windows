# Inert trigger switch looks active

Decision: triggers. Found in triggers review round 3, 2026-10-22.

A trigger switch can be On where it does nothing, such as "Use This Scene for Calls of Its Size" on a scene with no call size. Only a note under the switch says so. The rail chip looks the same as a working one. The owner reads the rail and expects the trigger to fire.

Fix: mark the switch itself ("On, inactive") and put the warning mark on its rail chip.
