    Keyboard
    • Tab to a window, then arrows move it one cell and Shift+arrows resize it.
    • Ctrl+Shift+arrows resize it from its top or left edge.
    • S or Shift+F10 opens Split Cell on the selected window, open half or empty cell. Shift+S or a double-click splits a window side by side at once. J joins a split cell. Tab reaches each empty cell and open half after the windows.
    • Delete unpins it. K keeps it where it is (off the board).
    • L locks or unlocks it. B brings the window behind it to the front.
    • A locked window moves only after a warning.
    • Ctrl+click cells to pick them, then Merge joins them.
    • Enter on a window that stays put opens it in the inspector, where Move To places it.
    • Draw Zone picks a zone's cells with the keyboard.
    • Esc cancels a drag, a resize or a zone you are drawing.
