    Keyboard
    • Tab to a window, then arrows move it one cell and Shift+arrows resize it.
    • Delete unpins it. K keeps it where it is (off the board).
    • L locks or unlocks it. B brings the window behind it to the front.
    • Enter on a window that stays put opens it in the inspector, where Move To places it.
    • Draw Zone picks a zone's cells with the keyboard.
    • Esc cancels a drag, a resize or a zone you are drawing.
