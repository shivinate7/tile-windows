# Merge grant

**Question.** Who may merge pull requests into main in this repo?

**Ruling (2026-10-22, owner).** Claude holds standing merge authority here. A change merges once it has passed the reviews the owner's rules require and its checks are green. The repo has no CI, so green means the test suite passes on the merge head. The owner can withdraw the grant at any time.

**Why.** The owner gave the word in session. It removes the wait for a merge word on reviewed, green changes.
